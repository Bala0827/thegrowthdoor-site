Agency operations in one workspace. Sample data.

Your team's work is not disorganised. It is scattered.

The problem is rarely effort. It is where the work lives.

Work spread across chats and sheets

Tasks are assigned in WhatsApp groups, tracked in spreadsheets and chased from memory. When someone is away, their work is invisible to everyone else.

No single view of who is doing what

A manager cannot see, in one place, what each team member is working on, what is stuck and what is waiting for attention.

Approvals stuck in chat threads

Work waits for a yes that was given, or missed, inside a long chat. There is no clear record of what was approved and what is still pending.

What Growmo does for your agency

Only what the system actually does today, listed plainly.

Employee Management

Your whole team in one directory, with each person's role and the work they own.

Task Creation and Assignment

Create a task, assign it to a person and set a deadline. Everyone works from their own clear list.

Team-Lead Approval Workflows

Submitted work goes to the team lead for review and approval, with a clear status at every step.

Task Progress Tracking

See what is pending, in progress, submitted and approved, without asking around.

Accounts Management

Invoices, expenses and payouts recorded in the same system as the work itself.

Roles and Permissions

Admins, team leads and team members each see what their role needs, and nothing they do not.

How a week runs on Growmo

Three steps, repeated for every piece of work.

1

Create and assign the task

The task is created once, with an owner and a deadline. Recurring work runs from task templates, so the weekly routine is set up once and repeats.

2

The team updates and submits

Team members update progress on their tasks and submit finished work for review, in the same place.

3

The team lead approves

The lead reviews the work, approves it or sends it back with comments. The status is visible to everyone who needs it.

Questions agencies ask us

Before you book the demo.

Is Growmo a sales CRM?

Growmo is an operations management system for agencies. It keeps lead records and connects with our lead tools, but its strength is organising your team, tasks, approvals and accounts. If you mainly need a sales pipeline tool, we will tell you honestly on the demo call.

Who is Growmo built for?

Digital marketing, SEO, web and creative agencies, and small service teams that assign and approve work every day.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Growmo runs in the browser, and your team signs in from their own devices. Your team's accounts are set up during onboarding.

Can it handle recurring weekly tasks?

Yes. Task templates and recurring tasks are part of how our own agency runs its week on Growmo.

Does Growmo include AI features?

Not in the current offer. Growmo focuses on organising your team's work, and we do not sell AI add-ons with it today.

How do we start?

Book a demo. We show you the system with sample data, understand your team structure, and confirm setup scope and pricing on the call.
