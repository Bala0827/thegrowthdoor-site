Questions, answered

Before you message us.

How much does it cost to start?

Start with what you need. A Google Business Profile setup is Rs. 1,999 one-time, a business website is Rs. 9,999 one-time, and the WhatsApp CRM is Rs. 1,999 per month after setup. The Growth Bundle combines all three at Rs. 24,999. Every price is published on our services pages.

How long before my website and profile are live?

A standard website takes 7 to 10 working days once your content is ready. A profile setup happens in one careful pass, after which verification time is Google's. The full Growth Bundle launches in 10 to 14 working days.

Do I own my website, profile and data?

Yes, completely. The domain, the website, the Google profile, the WhatsApp number and all customer data stay in your name. We work with access you grant, and you can remove that access anytime.

How soon will enquiries come?

Honestly, it depends on your market and your competition. The systems go live in days. Visibility then grows over weeks as reviews, content and rankings build. We show you the movement in plain reports, instead of promising dates nobody can guarantee.

Can you manage replies and reviews for me?

Yes. On the WhatsApp CRM, the assistant answers common questions instantly and your team handles the rest from one inbox. On our care plans, we also handle review responses and monthly profile upkeep for you.

What is AEO, in plain words?

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI for the best option near them, the AI names a few businesses. AEO is the work that makes sure your business is one of the names it gives: clear answers on your website, correct details everywhere, and the signals AI tools trust.
