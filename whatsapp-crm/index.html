Talk to Us

Questions, answered straight

Before you book the demo.

Do I have to change my WhatsApp number?

We help connect an eligible existing or new business number to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Number availability and migration options are confirmed during onboarding.

Is this the WhatsApp Business App?

No. The app lives on one phone, with linked devices for a small team. This is a shared team workspace on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, with WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and email in one inbox, and an assigned owner for every conversation.

What does Meta charge, and do you add anything?

Meta WhatsApp messaging charges are billed separately according to Meta's applicable pricing. Your payment method is used directly, with zero markup from The Growth Door.

Can it send offers and broadcast messages to my customers?

WhatsApp supports approved business-initiated message templates for eligible, opted-in recipients. Campaign availability and setup depend on the configured account and current testing status. We confirm this during onboarding.

Will AI reply to my customers automatically?

No. This is a team inbox, not an AI responder. Automation labels and assigns enquiries the moment they arrive, and your team replies personally, with saved one-tap responses for speed.

How long does setup take, and who does it?

We do the full setup for you: number connection, inbox configuration and a live test enquiry before go-live. Your exact timeline is confirmed during onboarding.
