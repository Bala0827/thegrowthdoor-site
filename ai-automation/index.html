Questions businesses ask us

Before you book the consultation.

How much does automation cost?

It depends on how many workflows you need and which tools they connect. We confirm one clear price after the discovery step, before any build starts. There is no published price list because no two businesses repeat the same work.

How long does an implementation take?

A single workflow is usually a matter of days once your tool access is ready; a set of connected workflows takes longer. Your design document states the timeline before we build, and we confirm it with you rather than promising a fixed number here.

Which tools can you connect?

Any tool with an API or webhooks is usually connectable, and n8n already supports hundreds of common business tools. We verify your exact tools during discovery and tell you plainly if one cannot be connected reliably.

Is the WhatsApp automation official?

Yes. WhatsApp workflows run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform through our Omnichannel CRM. Automated steps organise and route enquiries; your team still talks to customers. Meta's messaging charges are billed separately on your own payment method, with zero markup from The Growth Door.

What happens if a workflow breaks?

Every workflow we run is monitored, and failures raise an alert instead of failing silently. Support covers diagnosis and fixes, and the design step always includes what should happen when a step fails.

Is our business data safe?

Workflows are built with the minimum access each step needs, credentials are stored securely rather than inside workflow steps, and your data stays in your own tools and accounts. We confirm the access list with you during discovery.

Do you deliver automation under our agency's brand?

Yes. We design, build and maintain the workflows, and your agency stays the client's point of contact. How an engagement is branded, priced and contracted is agreed in writing before work starts. Use the Agency Partnership Enquiry button on this page to begin the conversation.
