Website Design Cost in Chennai: What a Small Business Actually Pays in 2026

10 November 2026 · By The Growth Door, Chennai · 6 min read

Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales

Ask three people in Chennai what a website costs and you will get three different answers, sometimes thousands of rupees apart. The word website covers very different things: a single page for one offer, a business site with your services and contact details, or a shop that takes orders and payments. This guide gives you the price bands Chennai small businesses actually pay in 2026, what each band includes, and the yearly costs that never appear in the first quote, so you can judge any quote in five minutes.

The short answer

If you run a small business in Chennai and need a standard website, a 5 to 7 page mobile-first site with your services, contact details, Maps and a WhatsApp button, expect to pay roughly Rs. 9,999 to Rs. 15,000 as a one-time cost in 2026. That is the band most honest quotes land in. A single page costs less, an online shop costs more, and anything custom is quoted per project. The rest of this guide explains why prices move inside those bands, and what to check before you pay.

What actually decides the price?

Five things move the price more than anything else. First, the number of pages: a five-page site costs less than a fifteen-page one. Second, the design: a ready template adapted to your brand costs less than a design drawn from scratch. Third, the content: who writes the words and supplies the photos. Hand over finished text and photos and the build is faster and cheaper. Fourth, the features: a contact form is simple, online payments and booking systems are not. Fifth, who builds it: a freelancer, an agency, or a do-it-yourself builder each price the same job differently.

The price bands you will see in Chennai

Here are the bands you will typically be quoted in Chennai in 2026. Treat them as guide ranges: scope moves every quote.

  • One-page site or landing page, Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 7,000. One page for one offer or event. Fine when you need a single link to send people to, not a full website.
  • Standard business website, 5 to 7 pages, Rs. 9,999 to Rs. 15,000. Home, About, Services, gallery, contact, plus WhatsApp chat and Maps. This is the right size for most salons, clinics, caterers, coaching centres and local shops. Our own business website sits at Rs. 9,999 one-time for 5 to 7 pages, and you can see exactly what it includes on our business website page.
  • Larger business website, 8 to 15 pages with a blog or detailed service pages, Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 30,000. The extra cost is content and structure, not decoration.
  • Online store, Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 60,000 and above. Products, payments, delivery settings and order handling. The range is wide because a 20-product shop and a 500-product shop are different jobs. Our ecommerce website page explains what goes into a store build.
  • Custom features or a web app, quoted per project. Booking systems, member logins, quote calculators and similar features are priced on scope, so any fixed number here would be guesswork.

The yearly costs nobody puts in the first quote

A website has running costs after launch, and a good quote tells you about them upfront. The domain name usually costs around Rs. 800 to Rs. 1,200 a year. Hosting usually costs around Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 5,000 a year for a small business site. Security certificates are usually free with decent hosting now, so do not pay extra for one without asking why. Then upkeep: content changes, new photos, the occasional fix. Some providers charge a monthly care fee for that. We do not charge a monthly fee for the website itself: once it is built, it is yours, and domain and hosting are extra at actuals only if you do not already have them.

What a very cheap website usually leaves out

A quote far below the bands above is not always a bargain. It usually means something was left out. The usual gaps: the site looks fine on a laptop but breaks on a phone, where most of your customers are. There is no Google Maps link, no WhatsApp button, and no enquiry form that actually reaches you. The text is placeholder copy nobody replaced. Sharpest of all: you do not own the domain or the files, so leaving later means starting over. And the follow-up gap: a website brings the enquiry in, but if nobody tracks it, the lead still slips away. We wrote about that gap in why your business is losing WhatsApp leads, and the honest numbers behind the fix in what a WhatsApp CRM costs in India.

How to compare two quotes in five minutes

When two quotes land on your desk, compare them on the same points:

  • Same page count and same features, or is one quoting less work?
  • Mobile-first design and fast loading, stated or assumed?
  • Who writes the content and supplies the photos?
  • Domain and hosting: included for the first year, or extra?
  • Do you own the domain, the files and the content outright?
  • WhatsApp chat, Maps and an enquiry form included?
  • Launch time in writing, and what support looks like after launch?
  • Can you see past work, live sites you can open on your phone?

If a quote cannot answer these plainly, the price is not the problem. The missing detail is.

Questions owners ask us

Short answers, before you message us.

How much does a basic business website cost in Chennai?

A 5 to 7 page mobile-first business website typically costs Rs. 9,999 to Rs. 15,000 one-time in 2026, plus domain and hosting at actuals. A one-page site costs less, and an online shop costs more.

Are domain and hosting included in the website price?

Not always, so ask before you agree. Some quotes include the first year and some charge separately. With us, domain and hosting are extra at actuals only if you do not already have them, and the website itself has no monthly fee.

How long does it take to build a small business website?

A standard 5 to 7 page site takes 7 to 10 working days once your content and photos are ready. Online shops and custom features take longer, because products, payments and testing add steps.
