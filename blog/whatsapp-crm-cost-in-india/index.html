How Much Does a WhatsApp CRM Cost in India?

8 November 2026 · By The Growth Door, Chennai · 6 min read

Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales✦ Leads → Growth → Sales

"How much does a WhatsApp CRM cost?" sounds like a simple question, but most pricing pages make it hard to answer, because a WhatsApp CRM is not one cost. It is three separate costs stacked together, and different providers bundle them differently, which is why two quotes for the same thing can look wildly different. We separate the three below, explain what Meta itself charges, and give realistic ranges for India in 2026, so you can compare any quote properly, including ours.

What are you actually paying for?

Every WhatsApp CRM cost in India has up to three parts:

  • The platform: the software itself: the shared inbox, pipeline, labels, team logins and reports. Usually a monthly subscription per business, sometimes per user.
  • Setup and management: connecting your number to the official API, building your pipeline and templates, training your team, and keeping it running. DIY platforms charge little or nothing here, because you do this work yourself. Done-for-you services charge for exactly this.
  • Meta's message charges: what WhatsApp's owner charges per template message, explained below. This is billed by Meta, whatever tool you use.

When two quotes look wildly different, it is usually because one includes all three parts and the other quietly includes only the first.

What does Meta charge for WhatsApp messages?

This part is fixed by Meta, not by your CRM provider, and it is simpler than it sounds. When a customer messages you, a 24-hour service window opens. Replies inside that window are service conversations, and the first 1,000 service conversations every month are free. For most small businesses, everyday customer replies cost nothing at all.

You pay Meta when you start a conversation outside that window using a pre-approved template message. In India, a marketing template, used for offers, promotions and broadcasts, costs roughly Rs. 0.86 per message, while a utility template, used for appointment reminders, order updates and similar notices, costs roughly Rs. 0.115 per message. Meta bills these charges directly. On a done-for-you service like ours, they go on your own card, with no markup and no platform fee added by us. Exact rates can change when Meta revises its pricing, so treat these as current guide figures, not a contract.

What do WhatsApp CRM platforms typically cost per month?

For the software alone, Indian small businesses in 2026 typically see DIY platform subscriptions roughly in the Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 2,500 per month range for starter and growth plans from well-known providers, with higher tiers above that adding more users, broadcasts or automation. At this price you get the tool. The setup work, API connection, pipeline design, template approvals, team training and day-to-day management, is yours to figure out.

That suits a business with someone in-house who enjoys configuring software. It suits most small teams less well: the tool is bought, the number is connected halfway, and three months later the team is back on personal WhatsApp because nobody finished the setup. The subscription was cheap. The outcome was not.

What does a done-for-you WhatsApp CRM cost instead?

The alternative model is done-for-you: a provider sets up and manages the system for a one-time setup fee plus a monthly management fee. Typical arrangements in India pair a setup fee, anywhere from a few thousand rupees to the low five figures depending on scope, with a monthly management fee in a similar band to a platform subscription. In return, the provider does the API connection, builds your pipeline stages and labels, prepares your templates, trains your team, and keeps the follow-up system maintained and reported on.

Our own pricing, stated plainly as one concrete reference point: Founding Offer, for our first 5 clients, setup Rs. 4,999 plus Rs. 1,999 per month. Standard pricing: setup Rs. 10,000 plus Rs. 3,000 per month. Meta's charges stay separate, billed by Meta directly on your own card. The system is built on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta API), and you can see everything the setup includes on our WhatsApp CRM system page. Compare it the same way you would any quote.

How should you compare two WhatsApp CRM quotes?

  • What is included in setup? Number connection, channels like Instagram, Facebook and website, pipeline, labels, quick replies, templates, training, or just an account login?
  • Who does the work each month? If the answer is your team, price in your own hours honestly.
  • Are Meta's charges passed through at cost? They should be billed by Meta directly, or itemised, never bundled opaquely.
  • What happens if you leave? Your number, contacts and history should remain yours, with no lock-in.
  • Is there a demo on your own enquiries? A provider confident in the outcome will show the system working before you pay.

Add those answers up and the cheapest sticker price often stops being the cheapest option. What you are really buying is not software. It is the follow-up happening every day without depending on anyone's memory. If you want the other half of the picture first, read why your business is losing WhatsApp leads.

Questions owners ask us

Short answers, before you message us.

Is there a per user charge?

Not with us. The whole team is included in one monthly plan, with individual logins and no per seat fee. Some platforms price per user, which is one of the first things to check in any quote.

Who pays Meta's message charges?

You do, directly. Meta bills your own card at actuals, and replies inside the 24-hour window are mostly free. We add no markup and no platform fee on top of Meta's charges.

Is there a long contract?

No. Our plan is month to month, with no annual lock-in. Your number, your contacts and your history remain yours.
