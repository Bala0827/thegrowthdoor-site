Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about App Development.

How much does an app cost?

It depends entirely on scope: a focused business tool and a full marketplace are very different builds. After a free discovery call you receive one fixed quote for a written scope. If it does not fit, the scope document is yours to keep.

Which technology do you build in?

The one that fits the job. WordPress for content-led sites, custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript for fast custom tools, and React or Next.js for full applications. We are deliberately not a single-technology shop.

Can you show me apps you have built?

Yes. FreelancerSpot, a live freelancer marketplace; Growmo CRM, a CRM platform used by institutes and agencies; and LeadDoor, a lead capture system. Message us on WhatsApp and we will walk you through them.

Do you maintain the app after launch?

Yes. Care plans cover updates, backups, monitoring and small improvements, so your tool keeps pace with your business.
