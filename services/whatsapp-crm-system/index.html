Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about WhatsApp CRM System.

Will my number stop working on my phone?

No. Your number works on your WhatsApp Business app and in the team inbox at the same time. Your app, chats and contacts stay exactly as they are.

Who pays the Meta message charges?

You do, on your own card, at Meta's actual rates. We add zero markup. The first 1,000 service replies every month are free from Meta.

How long does setup take?

Two to three working days after your approval, including templates and team training.

Is there a per user charge?

No. The system includes unlimited team members. Your cost does not rise when your team grows.
