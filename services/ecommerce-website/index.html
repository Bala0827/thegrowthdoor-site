Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about E-commerce Website.

Which payment methods can customers use?

UPI, debit and credit cards and netbanking, through the payment gateway connected to your store.

Can I add and edit products myself?

Yes. The admin dashboard is built for that, and we train you on it. We are one message away for anything unusual.

How many products can I start with?

Start with your core range and grow the catalogue as you learn what sells online. We load your initial range with you during the build.

Will orders also reach me on WhatsApp?

Order alerts and a WhatsApp enquiry option on product pages are included, so you never miss a serious buyer.
