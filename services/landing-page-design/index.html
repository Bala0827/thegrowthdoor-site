Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about Landing Page Design.

Why not send ads to my homepage or website?

A homepage serves every visitor and every service. An ad visitor came for one offer. One page, one offer, one button converts far better and makes tracking honest.

How fast can the page go live?

Three to five working days once the offer details and photos are with us.

I have two offers. Can I have two pages?

Yes, and that usually works better than mixing offers. Each additional page is at the same yearly rate.

Do you write the page copy?

Yes. We structure and polish the copy from your offer details, in language your customers use.
