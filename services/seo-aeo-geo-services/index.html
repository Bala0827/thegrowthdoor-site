Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about SEO, AEO and GEO Services.

What are AEO and GEO, in simple words?

AEO means structuring your content so AI assistants quote your business as the answer. GEO means building the brand signals across the web that AI tools use to decide who to recommend. Plain SEO alone no longer covers how customers search.

How soon will I see movement?

Early movement typically shows in six to ten weeks, depending on competition. We show the trend every month so you judge by data, not promises.

Is content writing included?

Core page content and answer content for your 10 keywords are included. Large blog programmes can be added if we agree they will help.

Why only 10 keywords?

Focus beats spreading thin. Ten keywords worked properly will outrank fifty keywords touched lightly.
