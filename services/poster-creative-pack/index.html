Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about Poster & Creative Pack.

How many posters do I get each month?

The set is fixed with you at the start of the month around your offers and festivals. Most months cover your weekly offers plus the month's occasions.

Can you match my existing brand look?

Yes. Share your logo and two or three designs you like, and we lock the style from there.

I need a poster today for a sudden sale. Possible?

The pack includes a priority slot for sale days. Send the offer early in the day and we turn it around on priority.

Do you also post the creatives?

Posting and captions are part of Social Media Marketing. This pack delivers the designs, ready to post, and many clients take both together.
