Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about Google Business Profile Setup.

I already have a profile. Is cleanup included?

Yes. Most setups are cleanups: fixing categories, services, photos and information on an existing profile.

How long does verification take?

Google decides the method and the time. Video verification is common for new profiles, and we guide you through the exact steps.

Will this put me first on Maps?

Setup gives you the correct foundation Google ranks on. Position then grows with reviews, photos and upkeep, which our monthly services continue.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. Everything is done with manager access that you grant and can remove anytime.
