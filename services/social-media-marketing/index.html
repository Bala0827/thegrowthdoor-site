Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about Social Media Marketing.

How many posts do I get?

The count is fixed with you in the monthly calendar, based on your offers and platforms. The calendar is approved by you before creation starts.

Which platforms are covered?

Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Most local businesses start with Instagram and Facebook, where their buyers already are.

Do you reply to comments and DMs?

Enquiries are routed to your WhatsApp and inbox systems so your team closes them. If you take the WhatsApp CRM, everything lands in one tracked inbox.

Can you also run our ads?

Yes, Meta ads management is available as an add-on, with your ad spend paid directly by you.
