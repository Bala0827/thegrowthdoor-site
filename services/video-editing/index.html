Questions, answered

The things business owners ask us about Video Editing.

What footage should I send?

Phone footage is fine. Vertical clips of your work, products, premises and customers (with permission) edit best. We will tell you honestly if a clip cannot carry a reel.

How long does one reel take?

Two to three working days per batch, depending on how many reels you send together.

Can you match my existing brand style?

Yes. Send two or three reels or posters you like, and we lock your colours, fonts and caption style.

Do you also shoot the videos?

This service is editing only. For many clients, phone footage plus professional editing is the fastest and most honest format.
